Investing

Investment approach and philosophy

Educational content to understand how we think about investing: patience, risk management and a global view.

Philosophy

Principles before predictions

Investing is not about guessing the market's next move. It is about building, with discipline and knowledge, wealth that withstands the test of time.

Our philosophy is based on principles, not predictions. We share this approach for educational purposes only, so you can form your own judgement and, if needed, consult an authorised financial adviser.

Approach

Four principles

Long horizon

Time is the patient investor's best ally. We think in years and decades.

Risk management

Understanding how much you could lose matters more than imagining how much you could gain.

Diversification

A global view reduces dependence on a single market, sector or asset.

Continuous learning

Investing without understanding is gambling. Knowledge is the first investment.

Our vision

Global minds.
Long-term thinking.

We think globally and long term. Opportunities know no borders, and great wealth is not built in a hurry.

That is why our content aims to give you context, perspective and tools to decide for yourself.

Transparency

What we do not do

  • We do not offer buy or sell signals.
  • We do not manage third-party money.
  • We do not promise returns or profit percentages.
  • We do not replace advice from an authorised professional.
